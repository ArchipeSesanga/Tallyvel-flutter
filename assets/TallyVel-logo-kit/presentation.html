What we heard

The brief

TallyVel helps South African stokvels track contributions, payouts and the rotation. It records the money; it doesn't move it. It must feel trustworthy, communal, transparent and warm.

trustworthycommunaltransparentwarm

Distinct from blue fintech marks and coin/arrow/shield clichés; readable at 16 px; works in one colour and reversed.

TallyVel — identity concepts2
Concept A

Counted

Four tally marks closed by a check: every contribution counted and confirmed.

  • The name in a picture: a tally, made trustworthy by the check
  • Green for growth and stability, gold for the shared pot — straight from the TallyVel UI
  • Built from one bar width, one radius and 45° strokes; a three-bar cut for 16–32 px
